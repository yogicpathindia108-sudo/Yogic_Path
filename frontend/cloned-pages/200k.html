BEFORE YOU APPLY

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Yoga teacher training in Kerala — your questions, answered.

Do I need yoga experience to join the 200 hour YTT in Kerala?

No prior teaching experience required. A regular personal practice (any style) for 6+ months is ideal. Beginners welcome; we'll meet you where you are.

What is the difference between RYT 200, RYT 300, and RYT 500?

RYT 200 is the foundational Yoga Alliance teaching credential. RYT 300 is the advanced credential for already-certified teachers. RYT 500 = 200 + 300 combined.

Is your yoga teacher training Yoga Alliance certified?

Yes — we are a Yoga Alliance Registered School (RYS 200 and RYS 300). Graduates can register globally as RYT 200 or RYT 500 teachers.

How is Kerala different from Rishikesh or Goa for yoga teacher training?

Kerala is the birthplace of Ayurveda and offers an integration of yoga with Ayurveda that other Indian regions can't authentically claim. Quieter and less touristy than Rishikesh or Goa.

What's included in the 200 hour YTT tuition?

All teaching, accommodation (twin shared), 3 sattvic Ayurvedic meals daily, course materials, certification fees, and Kerala transportation. Excludes flights and visa.

How do I get to Kerala? Which airport?

Trivandrum International Airport (TRV) is 40 minutes from our shala. We provide airport pickup for arriving students within the welcome window.

Is there a payment plan or scholarship available?

Yes — payment plans split tuition into 2 or 3 installments. Limited scholarships available for BIPOC and economically disadvantaged applicants.

What style of yoga is taught — Hatha, Vinyasa, Ashtanga, or Ayurveda yoga?

All of the above. Our curriculum integrates Hatha, Vinyasa, Ashtanga, and Yin alongside Ayurveda, philosophy, anatomy, and Sanskrit. Lineage-led, not style-rigid.
