About Yogic Path

A Note from Our Founder

45

Credentials: E-RYT 500 | Master’s in Yogic Science | Ph.D. Scholar | Yoga Alliance Continuing Education Provider (YACEP)
I’ve spent more than a decade teaching yoga, and somewhere along the way, I realised that teaching yoga is not about having all the answers. It is about learning to observe, question, and understand.
Every student comes with a different body, a different story, and a different way of experiencing the practice. That changed the way I teach — and eventually became the foundation of Yogic Path.
I wanted to create a space where we could honour the depth of traditional yoga while also asking modern questions about the body, movement, and how we actually teach.
Yogic Path is my way of bringing those worlds together — and creating teachers who don't just perform yoga, but truly understand what they are sharing.

Rohit Rawat – Founder & Lead Yoga | Teacher Training Educator I yogic path

What We Explore

Yoga is a vast tradition. We bring its practices, philosophy, and understanding of the body into one complete learning experience.

  • ASANA
  • ANATOMY
  • BIOMECHANICS
  • PHILOSOPHY
  • PRANAYAMA
  • SUBTEL BODY
  • TEACHING

ASANA

Hatha · Ashtanga · Vinyasa · Yin
From traditional Hatha to dynamic Vinyasa and Ashtanga, we explore different approaches to asana — their principles, purpose, and application.

Anatomy

Functional Anatomy · Physiology · ndividual adapt applied anatomy
Understand the body you're actually teaching — how it moves, adapts, and differs from student to student.

Biomechanics

Alignment Principles Joint Mechanics Mobility & Stability Load & Movement
Learn To Look Beyond The Shape Of A Posture And Understand What Is Happening Underneath It.

Philosophy

Yoga Sutras · Bhagavad Gita · Yogic Principles Self-Inquiry & Yogic Living
Go beyond the physical practice and explore the ideas that give yoga its depth and direction.

Pranayama

Breath Regulation energy management nervous system balance · focus and concentration
Explore classical breathing practices and understand how breath influences the body, mind, and practice.

Subtle Body

Mantra · Mudra · Bandha · Meditation
Work With The Less Visible Dimensions Of Yoga Through Sound, Gesture, Energetic Locks, Concentration, And Inner Practice.

Teaching

Sequencing · Cueing · Adjustments · Observation
Turn knowledge into teaching — learning how to see your students, communicate clearly, adapt intelligently, and build meaningful classes.
